## This week: **Disco / ABBA-style pulse**

**Time:** about 25 minutes  
**Goal:** keep the harmony simple, but make the **rhythm** do more of the work. This week is about short, crisp right-hand chord hits against a steady left hand.

### 1. Black-key warm-up — 4 minutes

Use only these right-hand notes:

**A♭ – B♭ – D♭ – E♭**

Play this:

**RH: A♭ → B♭ → D♭ → E♭ → D♭ → B♭**

Repeat it a few times.

Then change the order however you want, but keep the notes short and fairly rhythmic rather than dreamy.

Think more:

**da-da / da-da / da-da**

and less:

**loooooong floating notes**

---

## 2. Your four main chord shapes — 6 minutes

Play these in order:

**①** LH: **A♭ + E♭** | RH: **C + E♭ + A♭**

**②** LH: **E♭ + B♭** | RH: **B♭ + E♭ + G**

**③** LH: **F + C** | RH: **C + F + A♭**

**④** LH: **D♭ + A♭** | RH: **D♭ + F + A♭**

Then repeat from ①.

For the first few minutes, just hold each shape for four counts:

**1 – 2 – 3 – 4**

Press everything on **1**, hold, and move to the next shape on the next **1**.

Don't worry about groove yet. Get the hand positions comfortable first.

---

## 3. Now give it the disco rhythm — 5 minutes

This is the main skill this week.

Count out loud:

**1 & 2 & 3 & 4 &**

Your **left hand** presses the chord on **1** and holds it for the whole measure.

Your **right hand** plays short chord hits on every **&**.

So:

**Count:** 1 **&** 2 **&** 3 **&** 4 **&**  
**LH:** PRESS————————————HOLD  
**RH:** — HIT — HIT — HIT — HIT

For chord ①, that means:

**LH:** hold **A♭ + E♭**

while the RH repeatedly taps:

**RH:** **C + E♭ + A♭**

Four short hits.

Release the RH immediately after each one. Think **tap**, not **hold**.

Then change both hands to chord ② on the next **1**.

This may feel strange for a minute because your RH is deliberately playing **between** the numbered beats. That's called playing **offbeats**.

Once it clicks, it'll suddenly sound much more like actual disco/pop accompaniment.

---

## 4. Beat-only section — 7–8 minutes

Find a **disco or four-on-the-floor drum loop around 110–118 BPM**.

“Four-on-the-floor” just means the kick drum hits on:

**1 – 2 – 3 – 4**

Start by playing the exact pattern above:

**LH: A♭ + E♭ | RH: C + E♭ + A♭**  
**LH: E♭ + B♭ | RH: B♭ + E♭ + G**  
**LH: F + C | RH: C + F + A♭**  
**LH: D♭ + A♭ | RH: D♭ + F + A♭**

One measure each.

After a few loops, simplify the LH to single notes:

**A♭ → E♭ → F → D♭**

Keep the RH offbeat chord hits going.

Then for the last couple of minutes, occasionally **skip one RH hit**.

Instead of:

**& & & &**

try:

**& & — &**

That tiny missing hit can make the groove feel much more musical.

Don't add anything else yet.
