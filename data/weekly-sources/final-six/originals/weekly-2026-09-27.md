## This week: **Country / Americana — steady “boom-chuck” groove**

**Time:** about 25 minutes  
**Goal:** keep the left hand steady, make the right hand answer it, and get comfortable enough with the loop that you can start adding your own little melody.

This week builds on the same hand-independence you were using with the disco offbeats, but slower and much more relaxed.

### 1. Warm-up — 4 minutes

Use just these five right-hand notes:

**LH: — | RH: G → A → B → D → E**

Then come back down:

**LH: — | RH: E → D → B → A → G**

Play that slowly a few times.

Then try this:

**LH: — | RH: G → B → A → D**

Pause.

Then:

**LH: — | RH: E → D → B → G**

Don’t worry about speed. Try to make each little group sound like a sentence rather than an exercise.

---

## 2. Your four chord shapes — 6 minutes

Play these in order:

**①** LH: **G + D** | RH: **B + D + G**  
**②** LH: **D + A** | RH: **A + D + F♯**  
**③** LH: **E + B** | RH: **G + B + E**  
**④** LH: **C + G** | RH: **G + C + E**

Then go back to ①.

For the first few minutes, just press both hands together and hold:

**1 – 2 – 3 – 4**

Press on **1**.  
Hold through **2, 3, 4**.  
Release just before the next **1**.

Keep looping until the order feels familiar.

---

## 3. The country rhythm — 5 minutes

Now count:

**1 & 2 & 3 & 4 &**

This is the whole groove:

**1:** press LH  
**&:** release LH  
**2:** press RH  
**&:** release RH  
**3:** press LH  
**&:** release LH  
**4:** press RH  
**&:** release RH

So it alternates:

**LH — RH — LH — RH**

For chord ①:

**1:** LH: **G + D**  
**2:** RH: **B + D + G**  
**3:** LH: **G + D**  
**4:** RH: **B + D + G**

Everything is short. Don’t hold anything down.

Then do the same pattern on the other three shapes.

This style is often called **“boom-chuck”**: the low notes are the “boom,” and the chord is the “chuck.”

---

Start by playing exactly:

**LH: G + D | RH: B + D + G**  
**LH: D + A | RH: A + D + F♯**  
**LH: E + B | RH: G + B + E**  
**LH: C + G | RH: G + C + E**

One full measure on each shape.

Keep the rhythm:

**LH — RH — LH — RH**

After a few loops, make the left hand simpler:

**LH: G | RH: B + D + G**  
**LH: D | RH: A + D + F♯**  
**LH: E | RH: G + B + E**  
**LH: C | RH: G + C + E**

Now the left hand sounds more like a bass player.

For the final couple of minutes, occasionally stop playing the RH chord and use your warm-up notes instead:

**G – A – B – D – E**

Play only 2–4 melody notes, then go back to the chord groove.

That back-and-forth is the important part.

**LH: G → D → E → C**
